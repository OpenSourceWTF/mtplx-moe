**Goal:** Build and validate an explicit local `hy3-expert-q2` artifact that requantizes only the 15,168 routed records in the pinned local Hy3 affine-Q4 sidecar, preserves exactly `17,494,289,664` resident tensor bytes, and remains off by default unless structural, numerical, quality, and paired resource gates pass.

Keep Qwen online for CPU-only phases; only pilot, conversion, quality, and benchmark commands enter a fail-closed exclusive MLX window.
