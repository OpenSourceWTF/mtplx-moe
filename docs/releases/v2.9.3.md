# MTPLX 2.9.3

Long-context decode stops falling off a cliff past 128k, the mlx floor moves to a measurably faster release, and KV-cache quantization goes from broken to usable — with the remaining gap stated honestly.

## The long-context decode cliff is fixed

Past 131,072 prompt tokens the engine silently repaged dense decode into a cache layout that structurally excluded the packed verify kernel: verify fell onto a lazy fallback paying roughly 30x its normal round cost, and speculative decode collapsed to plain-AR speed. On an M5 Max 128 GB at 147k context, decode measured 12.0 tok/s before the fix and 16.3 after — 18.4 with prompt-copy speculation off.

- The dense-decode ceiling is now memory-aware: `MTPLX_SUSTAINED_DENSE_DECODE_MAX_CONTEXT=auto` budgets 15 percent of device RAM against the model's KV bytes per token, clamps to the model's context window, and floors at the old 131,072 literal so no smaller machine regresses.
- The resolution is announced once in the serve log, including whether the model supplied its own KV geometry (`MTPLX_DENSE_KV_BYTES_PER_TOKEN`) or fell back to the Qwen3.8 default.
- Operators can still pin any numeric ceiling, and an exported env now beats the profile instead of being silently stomped back.

## Faster stack: mlx floor raised to 0.32.2

A clean same-wheel A/B on an M5 Max measured mlx 0.32.0 → 0.32.2 at +29 percent decode and +41 percent prefill at 88k context, and +31 percent at 16k. The dependency floor moves to `mlx>=0.32.2` so existing installs converge on the faster stack instead of sitting on 0.32.0 forever. New pin-guard tests fail loudly if an environment or a shipped profile ever drifts below the receipted stack again.

## KV-cache quantization: from broken to usable

Honesty first: q8/q4 KV quantization was effectively broken. Turning it on crashed serving at warmup, and before that bug existed it could cost half your decode speed. 2.9.3 replaces the lane:

- The warmup crash is fixed.
- q4 now routes through the exact packed-quant kernel via a persistent quantized bank instead of re-dequantizing the entire prefix every round.
- The compiled verify bank now promotes quantized paged caches instead of dropping every verify round to eager execution.
- A pre-existing q4 numerics defect at head_dim 128 is fenced fail-closed; the shipped model family (head_dim 256) is exact.

Measured cost at 16k context on an M5 Max, versus quantization off: q8 about −4 percent decode, q4 about −19 percent — down from crash / −50 percent. That is not yet the zero-loss bar we hold ourselves to, so the toggles stay opt-in: q8 is a fair trade today for 2x context headroom, q4 is for capacity emergencies. One known limit we are still working: past the dense-decode ceiling, quantized decode still drops onto a slow routing lane; the cause is located and the fix is in progress for a follow-up release.

## See what the engine actually did

The decode cliff hid for months because fast lanes declined silently. That class of blindness is closed:

- Every request row now records packed-route bail counters and paged-adapter engagement, so a lane that stops engaging shows up in the request log instead of only in a benchmark weeks later.
- `MTPLX_ROUTE_DEBUG=1` prints one line per layer naming the attention branch taken and every gate input.
- Warmup failures log their full traceback instead of a one-line skip.

## Fixed

- The dynamic-offset paged verify kernel had never compiled since its introduction (a pointer-cast Metal bug hidden behind its own mask gate) and crashed at q_len above 5 once compiled; both are fixed. The kernel stays behind its opt-in gate pending a serve verdict, but the crash class is gone.
- A model that ships without KV-geometry metadata now shows itself in the serve log instead of silently mis-budgeting dense decode.

## Experimental, off by default

- `--scheduler-mode hyper`: the single-user manufactured-concurrency chassis (H0). Trajectory-sha-identical to serial in every gate pair.
- `MTPLX_NAX_TILE_ROUTE`: the first M5 tensor-unit (Neural Accelerator) attention kernel at decode shapes — +34 to +45 percent over the scalar wide-QL kernel at QL9 in gated walks.
- `MTPLX_ADAPTIVE_DTEMP`: acceptance-EMA adaptive draft-temperature controller.
- `MTPLX_CCOPY_BANK_ROUTE`: copy-speculation dispatch through the compiled verify bank.
- `MTPLX_PAGED_TAILMASK_ELIDE`: the resurrected paged kernel's mask-elide fast path.
- `MTPLX_FORKEV_TELEMETRY`: shadow pricing for speculative fork expected value.
